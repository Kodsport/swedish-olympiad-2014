\problemname{The Standing Desk}
Anna's mom complains that she \emph{sits} too much at the computer every
day. Well, Anna will fix this. She has decided to
\emph{stand} up at the computer instead!

But the computer screen is too high up! Anna needs to come up exactly
$x$ centimeters for ergonomic reasons. She has $n$ plates to help her. By placing
a number of them under each foot, she can come up exactly $x$ centimeters.
The plates under the right foot must thus have a total height of $x$ centimeters, and the plates under the left foot must
also have a total height of $x$ centimeters. What is the minimum total number of plates she needs?

\section*{Input}

First comes a line with the numbers $x$ and $n$ ($10 \leq x \leq 100$, $2 \leq n \leq
20$), how many centimeters Anna must place under each foot and
the number of plates, respectively.

Then comes a line with $n$ integers $h_i$ ($1 \leq h_i \leq 100$). There will always be
a solution.

\section*{Output}
To make it harder to guess the correct answer, you should output two integers $n_1$ and $n_2$,
the number of plates under each foot. Output the smaller number first, i.e. ensure that $n_1\leq n_2$.
If there are multiple solutions where the total number of plates is the same, you may choose any of them.
